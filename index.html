Assembly Notes

Step-by-step guidance for building and installing your drawers.

1. Drawer Box Assembly: Cut all drawer components per the dimensions above. Assemble by joining front, back, and sides with dados or rabbet joints. Slide the bottom panel into grooves (6-8mm from bottom edge). Ensure box is square before fasteners set.
2. Undermount Slide Installation: Mount slide cabinet brackets to cabinet sides at the heights shown in the Position Heights table. Ensure slides are level and parallel. Attach drawer brackets to drawer box bottom, maintaining the specified front setback distance.
3. Side Clearances: Verify -mm total clearance on each side of drawer box for slide operation. This is critical for smooth movement.
4. Drawer Front Attachment: After testing drawer operation, attach overlay fronts using adjustable front mounting brackets. The overlay fronts should extend -mm beyond the opening on all sides.
5. Final Adjustments: Most undermount slides offer 2-3mm of adjustment in multiple directions. Fine-tune drawer alignment after installation.
